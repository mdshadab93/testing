Publication Records

Journals, conferences, books and chapters, searchable in one place.

Supabase is not set. Open this file in Notepad and paste your Project URL and key in SB_URL and SB_KEY near the top of the script.
All filled filters must match. Month and dates are read from the date of publication (12-05-2024 is day-month-year; May 2024 also works). A record with only a month and year, or only a year, matches if any part of that month or year falls in the date range. Either date can be left empty.
Click a heading to sort. Click a row to see the full record.

Import from Excel

Any Excel or CSV layout works: columns can have any name or order. The app matches them to the selected type, and you can correct the matching before importing.
