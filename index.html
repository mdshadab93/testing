All filled filters must match. Month and dates are read from the date of publication (12-05-2024 is day-month-year; May 2024 also works). For patents they are read from the Patent Published Date / Granted Date. A record with only a month and year, or only a year, matches if any part of that month or year falls in the date range. Either date can be left empty.
Click a heading to sort. Click a row to see the full record.

Import from Excel

Choose your exported Excel file (for example all_data_backup.xlsx), or select several files together. Every sheet is detected automatically (Journals, Conferences, Books, Book Chapters) and imported in one go.

Any Excel or CSV layout works: columns can have any name or order. The app matches them to the selected type, and you can correct the matching before importing.
